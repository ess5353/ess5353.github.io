Cat Apartment — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

EnglishTürkçeDeutschEspañolFrançaisPortuguês (Brasil)

This policy explains what information Cat Apartment (the "Game", formerly "Kedi Apartmanı"), developed by Eyüp Sina Sirkeci (individual developer, Istanbul, Türkiye), collects, how it is used and how long it is kept. Data controller: Eyüp Sina Sirkeci, contact: eypsrkc@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for personal information such as your name, email or phone number, and there are no accounts. Your game progress (coins, cats, settings, language) is stored only on your device. Deleting the Game deletes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (on iOS only if you allow tracking), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, your interactions with ads, and crash and performance data, in order to serve, measure and protect ads. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent for personalized ads and can withdraw or change it at any time under Settings → Ad privacy settings in the Game. On iOS you are asked for tracking permission; the Game works the same if you decline, and you can change this at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, you see non-personalized ads.

How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites · Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

3. Photos

In the cat home you can create a photo of your companion and share it. The image is made on your device. If you choose "Save Image", iOS asks for permission to add it to your Photos; the Game can only add that image and cannot see your photo library. Nothing is uploaded to us.

4. Purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by the app store you downloaded the Game from. We never see or store your payment details. You can restore it on a new device with Settings → Restore purchases.

5. Third parties

We share data only with the service provider named above (Google AdMob) and only for the purposes described. This provider is required to protect user data at the same or an equal level as described in this policy and required by the App Store Review Guidelines. We do not sell data.

6. Retention and deletion

Game data on your device is kept until you delete the Game. Retention of advertising data is governed by Google's policies; you can reset your advertising identifier in your device settings. To request deletion of your data, email eypsrkc@gmail.com.

7. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR and Turkish law (KVKK) you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, object to processing and withdraw consent. Email eypsrkc@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. For data collected by our advertising partner you can also use Google's privacy tools.

9. Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the new version with its date on this page. If the language versions differ, the English version prevails.

10. Contact

Eyüp Sina Sirkeci · eypsrkc@gmail.com
